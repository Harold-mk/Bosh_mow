# T1, pass 1: objectives and chapters of a new subject

A student creates a subject in a study app and imports its files: books and slides in
PDF, notes in Markdown. From these files, and from what you know of these works and of
their field, propose the objectives of the subject, its split into chapters and its
glossary. The student reviews and corrects your proposal before it is used.

## Input

The JSON object between `<input>` and `</input>` gives:

- `subject`: the name of the subject, and `exam_date`: the date of its final exam;
- `files`: the imported files, each with its `id`, `name`, `kind` (`pdf` or
  `markdown`) and `page_count`. A Markdown file is cut into sections at its `#` and
  `##` titles, and each section counts as one page.

Each file comes with one of these, depending on what the app could read in it:

- `outline`: its own table of contents (PDF bookmarks or Markdown titles), as titles
  with their level and their page;
- `title_candidates`: lines that look like titles because of their size or weight,
  with a level and a page; some of them are not real titles;
- `page_starts`: the first lines of each page;
- `page_texts`: the whole text of each page.

Pages are numbered from 1 to `page_count` in each file, whatever number is printed on
them.

## Objectives

- Give 1 to 5 objectives: the major skills that the final exam checks.
- Each objective is one sentence in French that starts with « Être capable de », or
  « Être capable d' » before a vowel, and names a skill that an exam can check, for
  example « Être capable d'estimer et d'interpréter un modèle de régression
  linéaire ».

## Chapters

A chapter is a part of the subject that is studied, then evaluated, as a whole. Follow
the structure of the files: usually, one chapter of the main book, or one lecture, is
one chapter here. Put the notes of the other files with the chapter they belong to.

- List the chapters in the order of study, usually the order of the main book.
- `title`: a short title in French.
- `role`: `core` for a chapter at the heart of the objectives and of the exam,
  `secondary` for a complementary one.
- `sources`: for each file that the chapter uses, the file's `id` in `file` and a single
  range of pages, from `first_page` to `last_page`.
- `objectives`: the numbers of the objectives that the chapter teaches, 1 being the
  first objective. Each objective is taught by at least one chapter.

Together, the chapters cover every page of every file exactly once: no page left out,
no page in two chapters. The front matter of a file (cover, table of contents, preface)
goes in the range of its first chapter, and its back matter (appendices, bibliography,
index) in the range of its last chapter.

## Glossary

`glossary`: up to 30 technical terms of the field that stay as they are when the course
is translated into French, written as in the files, for example « p-value »,
« DataFrame » or « overfitting ».

## Answer

Answer with the JSON object only: `objectives` (the sentences), `chapters` and
`glossary`.
