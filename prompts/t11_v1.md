# T11: describe a page whose content is mostly in images

A student studies a course in a study app. This page of a course document, often a
slide, has little text: most of what it teaches is in images or diagrams. Describe it
in text, so that questions can be written from it later. The image of the page comes
with this message.

## Input

The JSON object between `<input>` and `</input>` gives the `page` number, the name of
the `document`, the title of the `chapter` that the page belongs to (null if unknown),
and the `text` that the app could extract from the page.

## Answer

- `page`: the page number given in the input.
- `description`: in French, 150 words at most, what the page shows and what it means:
  the kind of figure, what it compares or explains, its trends or its result. For
  example: « Courbe d'erreur en fonction de la complexité du modèle : l'erreur
  d'entraînement baisse toujours, l'erreur de test remonte après un minimum. »
- `elements`: the visible parts of the page, one short phrase each, in French: axes,
  curves, legends, labels, boxes, arrows.
- `to_check`: in French, what is unreadable or ambiguous on the page; null if
  everything is clear.

Describe only what is visible on the page: add nothing that is not there, even if you
know the subject well. What you cannot read, or are unsure of, goes in `to_check`,
never in the description. Keep formulas, code and technical terms as they are written.
